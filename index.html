The Reality of FX Trading

FX trading is notoriously difficult because it's a zero-sum game where retail traders compete against institutional giants and high-frequency algorithms with far superior data and speed. The high availability of leverage often lures traders into taking oversized positions, which can wipe out an account during a single volatile event. Beyond the technicals, the psychological toll is immense; the constant battle against greed and fear often leads to revenge trading or abandoning a proven strategy at the first sign of a drawdown, making consistent profitability an elusive goal for most.
